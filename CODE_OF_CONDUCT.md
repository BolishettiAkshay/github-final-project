# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in this project. We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, inappropriate comments, and other conduct that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related activities.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly and appropriately.

Project maintainers may take actions including warnings, removal of inappropriate content, or temporary or permanent restriction from participating in the project.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the circumstances and impact of each violation when determining an appropriate response. They will aim to handle reports respectfully, fairly, and confidentially.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is based on community standards and the Contributor Covenant, adapted for this project.
